Privacy Policy

Tivro — Chinese Learning App

The short version: Tivro is a 13+ app. We collect only what we need to make the app work. We do not sell your data. We do not show targeted ads. Your learning progress is stored on your device.

1. Who We Are

Tivro ("we", "us", "our") is a Chinese language learning app available on the Apple App Store. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: tivrohelp@gmail.com

2. Age Requirement

Tivro is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

3. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name — the display name you choose (you don't have to use your real name)
  • Email address — used to identify your account
  • Sign-in provider — whether you used Google, Apple, or email/password

We also store:

  • Learning progress — words learned, streaks, XP, mastered words
  • App preferences — your chosen HSK level, daily goal, settings

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any payment information.

Speaking practice: if you use the optional speaking practice feature, your short voice recording is sent securely to Microsoft's speech service to score your pronunciation in real time. The audio is processed immediately to produce your score and is not stored by us or linked to your identity.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save and sync your learning progress
  • To personalise your experience (e.g. recommended HSK level)
  • To send important account-related emails (no marketing without your consent)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

Tivro uses the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — account authentication, data storage. Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Sign in with Apple — optional sign-in method. Apple Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — in-app purchases processed by Apple. Apple does not share payment details with us.
  • Microsoft Azure Speech Services — scores your pronunciation during speaking practice. Audio is processed in real time and not stored. Microsoft Privacy Statement →

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs or behavioural tracking tools.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure). Your learning progress is stored primarily on your device and, if you use iCloud, backed up to your own iCloud account by Apple so it can be restored on a new device.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct — update your name or email in the app settings
  • Delete — delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing tivrohelp@gmail.com
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the app and request deletion at any time

If you are in the UK or EU, you also have rights under UK GDPR / GDPR including the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO at ico.org.uk).

8. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics may be retained longer.

9. Children's Privacy

Tivro is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

10. Cookies & Tracking

The Tivro app does not use cookies. Firebase may use device identifiers for authentication purposes. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

12. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests:

  • Email: tivrohelp@gmail.com
